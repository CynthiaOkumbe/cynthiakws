Kenya Wildlife Services

history of kenya wildldlife

Kenya Wildlife Services has been at the forefront of wildlife conservation in Kenya for several decades. The organization was established to protect and manage the country's rich biodiversity, ensuring the survival of its iconic species and their habitats.

mission and vision

The mission of Kenya Wildlife Services is to conserve and manage the country's wildlife and their habitats for future generations. The vision is to be a leading force in wildlife conservation and management in Kenya.
